# SecureVault user guide

## Create your vault
1. Open SecureVault and click **Get started free** (or **Create your vault**).
2. Enter your name, email and a **master password**. The meter turns green when it's strong (12+ characters, numbers, symbols, upper & lowercase).
3. Tick "I understand SecureVault can't reset my password" and click **Create secure vault**.
4. **Save your recovery key.** Download the `.txt` or copy it somewhere safe and offline (password manager, printed paper). It's the *only* way back in if you forget your password.

> Why can't SecureVault reset my password? Your files are encrypted with a key derived from your password, in your browser. We never have that key, so nobody — not even us — can decrypt your files without your password or recovery key.

## Log in on any computer
1. Go to **Log in**, enter email and master password.
2. If 2FA is on, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (or a backup code).
3. If you reload the page, your vault locks automatically; enter your password to **Unlock**.

## Upload files
- Click **Upload** and drop files into the box or **Browse files**, or
- drag files from your desktop **anywhere** onto the page — they go into the folder you're viewing.
Each file is encrypted on your device in 8 MB pieces; you'll see "Encrypting…", chunk progress and **Done**.

## Organise
- **New folder** creates a folder in the current location; open it by clicking.
- Drag a file card onto a folder to move it, or use **… → Move to…**.
- **… → Rename**, **Add to favorites** (⭐ shows in **Favorites**).
- **Recent** shows recently changed files. Use the **search box** to find files by name — search happens on your device.
- Switch between **grid** and **list** view at the top right.

## Preview & download
Click a file to open the details panel: images, PDFs, videos, audio and text files are decrypted and previewed right in your browser. Click **Download** to save the decrypted file.

## Versions
In the details panel, **New version** uploads an updated copy while keeping the old one. Under **Version history** you can download any version or **Restore** it as the current one.

## Share a file
1. Open the file → **Share**.
2. Optional: turn on **Password protection**, choose an **expiry** (1 hour … Never), a **download limit** and a private label.
3. Click **Create link**, then **Copy**. Send the full link (including the part after `#`) and, separately, the password.
4. The recipient opens the link, enters the password and the file is decrypted in *their* browser.
5. See and **revoke** all links under **Shared**. Links stop working automatically when they expire or reach the limit.

> We can't show a link again later, because its key is only in the URL you copied. Just create a new one.

## Trash
**Move to trash** hides a file or folder. In **Trash** you can **Restore** or **Delete forever**; **Empty trash** erases everything there and frees storage.

## Settings
- **Profile:** change your display name.
- **Security:** change your master password (other devices are signed out); set up **two-factor authentication** — scan the QR code, enter the code, and save your 10 backup codes; disable it with a code.
- **Devices:** see where you're signed in and **Sign out** any other device instantly.
- **Activity:** every sign-in (with "Sign-in alert"), failed attempt, upload, share and security change.
- **Appearance:** dark or light theme (also the ☀/🌙 button at the top).
- **Danger zone:** permanently delete your account and all files.

## Forgot your password?
On the login page click **Use recovery key**, enter your email, the recovery key and a new password. Your files stay intact; all devices are signed out.
